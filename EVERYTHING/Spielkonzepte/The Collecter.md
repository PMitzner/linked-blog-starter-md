# Rough Concept

The Collecter is a gridded top-down game with a stylized pixel art look in which the player is able to influence each block in certain ways with some restrictions.

In The Collecter you are always set on a day-night cycle within which you have limited energy at daytime similar to games like Stardew Valley.
Every action costs you a small amount of energy and the only way of replenishing is to go to bed.

First you arrive at a small city in an unknown land and you get taught the basics of making a living in this place.

You learn Farming being the predominant source of income. You can buy seeds at the seedhouse or occasionally with other traders. Some of these seeds are quite rare and special.

Second is Fishing, although the "fish" over here dont seem to behave or look as such. Honestly if they werent such a high price it wouldnt even be worth it.

Then you learn how to catch insects with a net. Putting down lures is quite effective too. Although some of the smarter bugs will still be no easy deal. It is worth tho!

Mining is next. Although very exhausting it can pay off extremely well and sometimes you find ancient badges along the way!

These are the basic actions which make use of their associated tools:
**Watering can**, **fishing rod**, **bug net**, **pickaxe**.
There is also the **axe, shovel** and the **hoe**.

Now, there will also be some other hidden ***Action Gear*** which will unlock new ways of collecting stuff!

One of these is going to be the **Treasure Gloves** which are used to open different kinds of secret Chests! Chests in this game are alive and tricky. You might want to use these special gloves to force these things open.

Next you will find out about **diving**. Using the **diving suit** you will be able to go underwater for a limited amount of time. While you are underwater, you will have the chance to grab some unique critters and such aswell as chests, loot bags, badges and more!
Be careful tho as there might also be some aggressive creatures appearing.

Of course you might have to defend yourself better. Look no further than the "**Sword**". This item will allow you to fight off anything that comes your way. While it does certainly not look like a sword and neither acts like one it does provide you with the fighting power to defend yourself.

Fights in this game are **turn-based**. You select a move every turn. Some moves are provided by your "sword" but there will be other items that provide combat usage and therefore appear in the move selection. Some of these items will be **food**, **potions,** other **weapons** and **gadgets**, which will provide different reusable utillity in fights.

#### Leveling and Moves

Defeating virtually anything in a battle will grant experience and level you up, every Levelup is significant. Most stats are increased and it lets you choose an upgrade as an additional reward from the following:

- 5 Health points (quite scarce since you start with only 10)

- 10 Maximum energy (starts at 40, every action requires energy)

- 3 Badge Points (starts at 3, used to activate Badges which come with special effects and advantages both in battle and on the outside world)

- 5 Special Points (starts at 10, this is the main in-combat ressource to use for special attacks and moves. May be regenerated by food or potions)

The Player will come across different Special Moves to learn. Following side-quests will often reward you with these. In Combat these will grant you all kinds of effects and/or attacks. Some Special Moves are tagged Legendary, those are the most powerful ones, sometimes consuming a large amount of SP (Special Points)

#### Swordcrafting

After completing the blacksmith quest the player will gain access to the **anvil** in which the player will be able to craft weapons from different materials.
Here the player needs only to put one bar of any eligible material down aswell as a handle and a bug.
Yes a bug, every sword can optionally be induced with an insects life. Every insect will have different effects when applied to a weapon. Swordhandles can be found while fishing, exploring, diving, mining or just as drops from enemies.

#### Toolcrafting

Toolcrafting works very similarly to Swordcrafting. The only difference being that you may require additional ingredients such as a string for the fishing rod and bow
