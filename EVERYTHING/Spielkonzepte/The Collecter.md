# Rough Concept
The game is a gridded top-down pixelart game where the player is able to influence each block in certain ways with some restrictions.

You arrive at a small city in an unknown land and you get taught the basics of making a living in this place.

First you learn Farming, being the predominant source of income. You can buy seeds at the seedhouse or occasionally with other traders. Some of these seeds are quite rare and special.

Second is Fishing, although the "fish" over here dont seem to behave or look as such. Honestly if they werent such a high price it wouldnt even be worth it.

Then you learn how to catch insects with a net. Putting down lures is quite effective too. Although some of the smarter ones will still be no easy deal. Its worth tho!

Mining is next. Although it is very exhausting, it can pay off very well and sometimes you find ancient relics
