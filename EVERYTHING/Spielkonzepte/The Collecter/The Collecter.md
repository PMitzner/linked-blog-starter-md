# Rough Concept

The Collecter is a gridded top-down game with a stylized pixel art look in which the player is able to influence each block in certain ways with some restrictions.

In The Collecter you are always set on a day-night cycle within which you have limited energy at daytime similar to games like Stardew Valley.
Every action costs you a small amount of energy and the only way of replenishing is to go to bed.

First you arrive at a small city in an unknown land and you get taught the basics of making a living in this place.

After aquiring some basic money making methods you get introduced to the trading system and how to optimize for maximum profit

The first person you meet, teaching you the basics is eeri, an alien fairy looking creature with a kind attitude. Eeri teaches you all you need to know in order to start out well. You even get some starting gear for free! 
Eventually you introduce yourself to your neighbours and other travellers. Talking to some you find out that there is supposedly a large amount of rich treasures hidden underneath this place.

Thats right. You remember why you even came here.
Goldstein was advertised as a magical rich and adventurous place. You never heard of those treasures before tough youre excited to go and find out yourself.

Now you realize that there is several missions you percieve:

Making lots of money to maximize your gear and the towns aesthetical richness

Venture into the depths of the unknown caves and underground systems to locate and return the treasures hidden within

Strengthen up and build a strong force to fight whatever lies in the dark depths of Goldstein 

Recover the beauty of Goldstein and bring love and peace to all inhabitants

And to not forget...

Find out about whatever all those adventurers are so badly trying to find here...






Down in the depths you will every now and then come across an ancient demon god who you may need to fight in order to pass and revieve the goodies. Some are smaller weaker demons but some are also ancient legendary menaces so be careful and use your ***sense*** to see strong forces nearby.
