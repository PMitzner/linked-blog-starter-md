Items are what constitudes to the set of main tools and utility used to interact with the world

Unlocking new items will unlock new interactions on the overworld and with that new areas and secrets.

## Items list

- hammer
- sift
- rod
- watering can
- gloves (also weapon)
- diving suit


## interactions list

- breaking rocks > hammer
- watering plants watering can
- sifting sand/water
- breaking crates / barrels / other stuff > hammer
- fishing > rod
- open chests > gloves
- dive into water > diving suit
